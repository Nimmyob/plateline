Words are only half the answer.

A literal translation gives you the words on the board. It doesn’t tell you whether a dish is fiery, meat free, or made with an ingredient you avoid. Plateline answers those questions for every dish before you order.

You came for the food, not the guesswork. Know what each dish is, how spicy it is, and what’s in it before you order.

Questions, answered

What is Plateline?

A mobile app that translates handwritten menus and stall boards from a camera photo.

What does it show for each dish?

What the dish is, its spice level, and whether it’s vegetarian, vegan, or contains common allergens.

Who is it for?

International tourists and food travellers, and anyone new to a city who can’t read the local language or script, including migrants, students, and expats.

Can it read messy handwriting?

Handwritten menus and stall boards are what Plateline is built for. {{accuracy wording}}

Can I rely on the allergen info?

Plateline shows whether a dish contains common allergens as a guide, not a guarantee. If you have a serious allergy, always confirm with the vendor.
